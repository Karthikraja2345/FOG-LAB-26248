# Automated After-Action Review (AAR): FOG-LAB 26248

## 1. Structure of the 14-Section AAR
1. **Exercise Summary**: Scenario package metadata, duration, participants, and high-level objectives.
2. **Information Environment**: Timeline of degradation injects, channel health, and communication occlusions.
3. **Team Timeline**: Chronological narrative of major tactical events and injects.
4. **Individual Timelines**: Role-specific audit trail of messages, acknowledgements, and information deliveries.
5. **Decision Context Cards**: Deep dive into every submitted decision with decision-time evidence vs post-exercise truth.
6. **Contradiction Analysis**: Heatmap and status of divergent sensor claims (Source A vs Source B).
7. **Information Asymmetry Matrix**: Cross-tabulation of roles versus intelligence sources showing who knew what and when.
8. **Coordination Analysis**: Communication density, role cross-talk, and acknowledgement latencies.
9. **Uncertainty Inventory**: Budget metrics tracking unresolved conflicts, stale telemetry, and dropped feeds.
10. **Outcome Reconciliations**: Post-exercise consequences linked to specific decision choices.
11. **Deterministic Replay Hook**: Direct link into replay scrub controls for step-by-step playback.
12. **Instructor Notes**: Freeform manual annotations by staff college evaluators.
13. **Export Engine**: One-click generation of standalone HTML packages, printable PDF reports, and JSON datasets.
14. **Instructional Disclaimer**: Mandatory legal and training notice confirming non-operational simulated context.
